# Rama — independent verification work, fixed price

We check whether the evidence your agent system produces can be verified by someone who does not trust you. You get a written report. We do not certify anything.

## 1. Conformance review — USD 1,200

The basic action-ref check (8 frozen vectors, run as a GitHub Action from the Composable Evidence Criteria lab) is free and you can run it yourself in minutes. This engagement is the work around it: we identify which profiles of the public corpus apply to your implementation, build the adapter, run them, and write up each failure with its cause, pinned to the exact corpus version and commit. The corpus stays free and public; you pay for the review and the report, not for access.

## 2. Third-party verifiability review — USD 1,200

You point us at one receipt, log or evidence format that your own system already publishes. Working only from what an outside party can obtain, with no credentials and no private access, we recompute what can be recomputed and report three things: what a third party can verify without your help, what it has to take on your word, and what the artifact appears to claim but does not commit to. One format, one report. We review only formats published by the client itself, never a third party's.

## What is the same for both

- Fixed price, the same for every client. No discounts and no private quotes.
- Delivered in 10 business days from your written confirmation of scope.
- Nothing is invoiced until you have confirmed scope, price and deliverable in writing.
- Payment in USDC on Base, or by invoice with 15 days to pay.
- The report is yours and confidential. We do not cite your results without your written consent.
- Every report says, in these words, that it is not a certification, endorsement or conformity seal by Rama, by the Composable Evidence Criteria lab or by LFDT. You may not present it as one.
- If the work finds a gap in the public corpus itself, that gap is published to the corpus like any other. Paying clients get no advance notice.
- Where a gap we find could be closed by an external anchor, the report says so. Our own anchor registry is public and free to use; it is not part of the price.
- Out of scope: changes to your code, remediation, ongoing monitoring, legal or compliance opinions.
